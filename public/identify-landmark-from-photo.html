Photo checklist

Give the identifier something specific to read

A famous silhouette may be enough. Lesser-known landmarks usually need text, distinctive details, or location context.

Strong landmark photo

  • The entire subject fits in the frame.
  • The camera faces the sign or plaque head-on.
  • Names, dates, symbols, and inscriptions are sharp.
  • The base and surrounding architecture remain visible.
  • Daylight separates details from the background.

Retake this photo

  • Only a small cropped detail is visible.
  • A person, tree, or vehicle blocks the main subject.
  • Glare or motion blur hides the text.
  • The photo is taken at an extreme angle.
  • A common replica has no location clues around it.

Common questions

Landmark identification FAQ

Can I identify a statue from a photo?

Yes. Use a front-facing image that includes the figure, pedestal, plaque, and surrounding location. SlapTrip can describe a depicted person and label the certainty of that identification.

What if the landmark result is uncertain?

Retake the photo closer and head-on. Include readable text or a distinctive architectural detail, and avoid heavy cropping, darkness, or obstruction.

Do I need to install an app?

No. SlapTrip runs in a modern web browser and accepts a new camera photo or an image from your library.

Where are my uploaded photos stored?

New analysis photos are kept on the current device for your account rather than saved in the synced analysis-history record. Read the privacy policy for details.
